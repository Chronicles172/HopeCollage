HOPE COLLEGE — Image Assets Folder
====================================

Place the following two files in this folder:

1. favicon.png
   - This is the small icon that appears on browser tabs.
   - Recommended size: 32x32 px or 64x64 px (square, PNG format)
   - Used in: ALL pages (via includes/layout.php)

2. logo.png
   - This is the school crest/logo shown in the navigation bar.
   - Recommended size: 100x100 px or larger (square, PNG format)
   - Used in: Navigation bar on ALL pages (via includes/layout.php)

Once you place both files here, they will automatically appear across the entire site.
